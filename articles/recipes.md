# Recipes

The [tutorial](https://lenarddome.com/psp/articles/tutorial.md)
introduces parameter space partitioning on a small model. Each recipe
applies it to a published model and analysis, and shows techniques that
carry over to your own models.

[![The six category structures of Shepard, Hovland and Jenkins drawn as
cubes](recipes/alcove.png)](https://lenarddome.com/psp/articles/recipe-alcove.md)

### ALCOVE and the six category structures

A slow learning model with four parameters: patterns with ties, planning
and saving a long run, reading a partition you cannot plot, and
robustness checks.
